Pursuing Live Event Technology

I’m a Software Engineering student at Thaddeus Stevens College of Technology pursuing a career in live event technology, with hands-on experience in live audio, A/V systems, and technical troubleshooting.

I’m especially interested in live events, FOH audio, theater, and the technical side of event production, and I’m looking to continue building experience in the field.
